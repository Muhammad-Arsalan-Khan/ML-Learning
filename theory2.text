cluster
Maan lijiye ek school mein 12 students hain. Har student ki height aur weight ka data diya gaya hai, lekin unke groups nahi bataye gaye. Machine Learning ka clustering algorithm students ki similar height aur weight dekh kar unhein alag-alag groups mein divide kar deta hai.
Cluster 1: Chhoti height aur kam weight wale students.
Cluster 2: Medium height aur medium weight wale students.
Cluster 3: Lambi height aur zyada weight wale students.

Mean 
Mean ka matlab hota hai average.
Mean = (2 + 4 + 6) ÷ 3 = 4
Yani 4 in numbers ka average hai.

Centroid
Centroid kisi cluster ka center point hota hai.
x ka mean = (2 + 4 + 6) ÷ 3 = 4
y ka mean = (3 + 5 + 7) ÷ 3 = 5
Is liye Centroid = (4, 5)

Euclidean(Distance)
Euclidean Distance do points ke darmiyan seedha (shortest) distance hota hai.
K-Means mein har data point se centroid tak distance nikala jata hai. Jis centroid ka distance sabse kam hota hai, point usi cluster mein chala jata hai.

formula --> distance = √(x2 - x1)**2 + (y2 - y1)**2
example 
Point A = (2, 3)
Point B = (5, 7)
√(5 - 2)**2 + (7 - 3)**2  ans ---> 5


varicance(spread)
Yani variance batata hai ke data ki values apne mean(average) se kitni door hain.
Variance kam → Data mean ke qareeb hai.
Variance zyada → Data mean se door phaila hua hai.
μ = mean
Variance= ∑(x1−μ)2​ + (x2−μ)2 + (x3−μ)2​ ​ (xn−μ)2​ / n
hum phlay variance ka squar root le gay phr os divide kary gay mean se phr 100 se multiply simple phr nichy rule hain agar variance mean ky 10% ky under hota hain tu low variance
V < 10% → Low variability
10%–20% → Moderate variability
20%–30% → High variability
> 30% → Very high variability

Population Variance
Population ka matlab hota hai "Saare ke Saare log."
Agar tum school ke sab ke sab 1,000 bachon ki height napo (measure karo), aur phir uska variance nikalo, to usko hum bolenge Population Variance.
formula --> sigma**2 =  ∑(x1−μ)2​ + (x2−μ)2 + (x3−μ)2​ ​ (xn−μ)2​ / n

Sample Variance
Sample ka matlab hota hai "Sab mein se chune hue kuch log."
Ab socho, 1,000 bachon ki height napna bohot mushkil kaam tha. To tumne chalaki ki! Tumne poore school mein se randomly sirf 50 bache chun liye. In 50 bachon ko hum bolenge "Sample"
Jab tum sirf in 50 bachon ki height ka variance nikaloge, to usay hum bolenge Sample Variance.
formula --> S**2 =  ∑(x1−μ)2​ + (x2−μ)2 + (x3−μ)2​ ​ (xn−μ)2​ / n - 1

PCA (Principal Component Analysis)
Maan lijiye har student ka data hai: Height,Weight,Age,Shoe Size,Marks
Agar Height aur Weight ek doosre se kaafi related hain, to PCA in dono ko mila kar ek naya feature bana sakta hai.
Yani:
Principal Component 1,Age,Shoe Size,Marks
pca
Data collect karta hai.
Mean nikalta hai.
Variance check karta hai.
Jis direction mein sabse zyada variance hota hai usay Principal Component 1 (PC1) banata hai.
Doosri important direction ko PC2 banata hai.
Sirf important components ko rakh kar dimensions kam kar deta hai.
PCA (Principal Component Analysis) ek dimensionality reduction technique hai jo original features ko naye principal components mein convert karti hai, taake important information ko preserve karte hue data ka size kam kiya ja sake.
PCA = Less Features + Same Important Information

** kmean cluster **
1- K ki value select ki jati hai, yani kitne clusters (groups) banane hain.
2- Random centroids select kiye jate hain, jo har cluster ka shuruati center hote hain.

4- Har cluster ka average (mean) nikala jata hai, aur us ki bunyaad par naya centroid banaya jata hai.
5- Ye process bar bar repeat hota hai jab tak centroids ki position change hona band na ho jaye aur clusters final na ho jayein.
